## 📊 Project Overview

Banking Customer Churn Analytics is a data analytics project designed to analyze customer behavior and identify patterns related to customer churn in the banking sector.

The project focuses on customer demographics, churn trends, product usage, customer segmentation, and the financial impact of churned customers. An interactive Power BI dashboard is created to present key insights and support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Analyze customer churn behavior
- Identify important factors affecting customer churn
- Analyze churn based on country, gender, and age group
- Study customer product usage and churn patterns
- Estimate the balance and revenue impact associated with churned customers
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide actionable insights for customer retention

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides insights into:
- Total Customers
- Customer Churn Rate
- Churned Customers
- Estimated Churned Balance
- Estimated Revenue Loss
- Country-wise Customer Churn
- Gender-wise Customer Churn
- Product Usage vs Customer Churn
- Age Group-wise Customer Churn
- Customer Segmentation by Age Group
